# SalesFinance

Sales and finance (`P12`, `P14`): customer Sales Order (SO) intake/review, available-vs-backorder stock split, explicit buyer acceptance, billing-only full-order Sales Invoice (`SI`), dual 48-hour unpaid reservation and backorder deposit clocks, funded shortage RepF forwarding, and late-payment availability resolution. Invoices never authorize stock release; payment verification & OR issuance remain Finance-gated.
