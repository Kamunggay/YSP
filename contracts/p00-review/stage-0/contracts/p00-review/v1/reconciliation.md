## Exact values and holds

IDs and quantities stay exact strings; versions use the shared bounded version definition. Quantity/unit valuation uses six-place decimals and approved unit scale, posted PHP money two places. UTC microsecond instants retain distinct occurrence, verification, entry and posting meanings with Asia/Manila display. The fixture's 25.000000 valuation is not a posted PHP money value or a chapter-approved valuation. No float conversion or rounding repairs an invalid source.

[Stock coverage](../../stock-holds/v1/coverage-model.md) uses identifiable disjoint cells, active-hold union and a once-counted held/reserved intersection. From the accepted Q1 example: on-hand 10, RF 4, SO 3, held union 4, held-reserved intersection 2 gives available 1; 10 > test ROP 8. Identical holds on three units give available 7, clearing only one stays 7, clearing the last gives 10 unless a reservation/restriction persists. Partial overlap A=3/B=2/shared=1 gives union 4 and available 6; clear A gives 8. These are separate hypothetical states, not successive observed transactions after the opening receipt. [Cases](../../stock-holds/v1/cases.json) and [race/failure schedules](../../stock-holds/v1/verification-cases.json) retain the complete expected cases, including protected commitments and quarantine.
