# Stage 2 canonical operation agreement

Specification: [#27](https://github.com/Kamunggay/YSP/issues/27), tickets `stage2-contracts-01`, `stage2-contracts-02`, `stage2-contracts-03`, and `stage2-contracts-04`. Candidate for Efren's consequential-boundary review and Glenn's UI/query consumer review; automated contract validation does not constitute their human sign-off. Source PRD, TRD, ADR 0001, ADR 0004, ADR 0005, `CONTEXT.md`, and `GLOSSARY.md` retain their existing authority. These slices deliver the canonical Stage 2 OpenAPI 3.1.1 baseline, controlled policy/evidence/reference extensions, live net-shortage query, internal shortage participant, RepF intake/duplicate-guard/budget-review lifecycle, nested quotations, policy-gated supplier selection, Purchase Order lifecycle/amendments/short-closure/cancellation, procurement action-receipt recovery routes, Stage 1 `cancelInternalRf` and `/stock*` `inbound_open_po` compatibility rules, Donation Acknowledgment and donor-restriction resolution lifecycle, worked examples, and validator only. They add no Laravel runtime code, database migrations, React UI screens, or CI workflow changes.

`openapi.yaml` is the single canonical Stage 2 HTTP and participant schema definition. `examples.yaml` binds every operation, controlled policy procedure, and internal participant to machine-validated payloads. Later tickets (`05`–`08`) extend these same files incrementally.

## 1. Shared Stage 1 conventions reused verbatim

All Stage 2 routes live under `/api/v1/chapters/{chapter_id}/...` and inherit Stage 1's settled transport and authority rules without redefinition:

| Concern | Verbatim Stage 1 rule carried into Stage 2 |
| --- | --- |
| Session & CSRF security | Same-origin `ysp_session` cookie (`browserSession`) on all protected routes; exact configured `Origin` plus URL-decoded `X-XSRF-TOKEN` (`csrfProof`) on all unsafe methods. No bearer tokens or client-selectable roles. |
| Caching | `Cache-Control: no-store` on every Stage 2 response, including errors, `204` responses, and printable HTML views. |
| Idempotency & replay | Unsafe Stage 2 mutations require `X-Action-Key` UUID scoped to `chapter + immutable actor + operationId` with canonical validated payload fingerprinting, emit `X-Action-Replayed: true|false`, and return `409 action_key_conflict` on changed-payload reuse. Safe `GET` routes accept no body and no `X-Action-Key`. |
| Pagination | `limit` integer `1..100` (default `25`), opaque 5-minute snapshot-bound `cursor` (`page: {next_cursor: string|null, snapshot_version: integer}`). Expired or changed snapshot returns `409 snapshot_stale`; unknown, repeated, or malformed selectors return `400 invalid_selector`. |
| Scalars & closed objects | Lowercase `Uuid`, UTC `UtcInstant` (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.ffffffZ`, displayed in `Asia/Manila`), canonical nonnegative `StockQuantity` matching exact approved variant scale `0..6` (zero silent rounding), `PhpAmount` (`0.00` two-decimal PHP), closed schemas (`additionalProperties: false`). |
| Error & blocker envelopes | Closed `Error`, `ValidationError` (RFC 6901 request-relative field pointers), `SetupBlocked` (`error.blockers: SetupBlocker[]`), and `Stage2Conflict` (`failed_intent_committed: false`, authorized `current` or `null`, `setup_blockers`). Uniform `404` (`chapter_not_found` / `resource_not_found`) after current chapter admission. |
| Donor restrictions | Verbatim Stage 1 `BoundedRestriction` (`unrestricted`, `bounded`, `uninterpreted`) per ADR 0004. |

Zero public HTTP policy-mutation routes exist in Stage 2. Every Stage 2 policy is governed exclusively through Stage 1's controlled CLI `recordPolicy` procedure with attributable `CHAPTER_HEAD` (operational) or `FINANCE` (financial) approvals and optimistic `expected_version`. Missing, unapproved, not-yet-effective, or expired policies block only their affected operations with `409 setup_blocked` (`SetupBlocker[]`)—never silent defaults.

## 5. Net-shortage & ROP replenishment contract (`GET /replenishment/needs` & `computeNetShortageSnapshot`)

### Authorization and query rules

- Requires full individual session + MFA, active membership in `{chapter_id}`, and current `replenishment.read` duty (`INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`, `BUDGET_APPROVER`, or `CHAPTER_HEAD`). Insufficient duty returns `403 duty_denied`; non-member/unknown chapter returns uniform `404 chapter_not_found`; unknown/foreign `item_id` or `variant_id` returns `404 resource_not_found`.
- Allowed query selectors are strictly `limit`, `cursor`, `item_id`, `variant_id`, `trigger_filter` (`all | rf_shortage | rop_breach | uncovered_only`, default `all`), and `q`. Unknown, repeated, or invalid selectors (including Stage 3's `so_shortage`) return `400 invalid_selector`.
- Ordering is deterministic: `item_code` ascending, then `variant_code` ascending, then `variant_id` ascending. Pagination binds a 5-minute snapshot cursor to actor, chapter, authority version, normalized filters, limit, and `snapshot_version`; stale or expired cursors return `409 snapshot_stale`.
- When `trigger_filter=rop_breach` or when `require_rop_and_target_policies=true` (used by Leader for `rop_replenishment` RepF writes), missing or expired `rop` or `target_level` for the requested scope returns `409 setup_blocked` (`SetupBlocked`). Missing or expired `quantity_scale` for any evaluated variant always returns `409 setup_blocked`. For `trigger_filter=all` or `rf_shortage`, missing `rop`/`target_level` appears in the item's `setup_blockers` array without hiding an active approved RF shortage (`INV-10`).

### Exact non-overlapping calculation (`INV-10`–`INV-12`, `PRC-02`)

All arithmetic uses exact decimal math at the variant's approved `quantity_scale` (never floating point). Stage 1 defines `rop` and `target_level` at `subject_scope: item` (`unit_id`, `quantity`). For single-variant items (the common chapter case), item usable stock equals the variant's `usable_stockroom_on_hand`. For multi-variant items sharing the policy's `unit_id`, runtime aggregates item-level usable stock and open inbound across sibling variants sharing that `unit_id` when evaluating item-level ROP breach and item-level target top-up so multiple variants of the same item never multiply `approved_target_level`, while each variant continues to surface its own exact `unreserved_approved_rf_shortage`:

1. `eligible_available_to_allocate = max(0, eligible_usable_stockroom_on_hand - active_rf_reservations)`
2. `unreserved_approved_rf_shortage = max(0, total_unreserved_approved_rf_demand - eligible_available_to_allocate)`
3. `low_stock_breach = (approved_rop != null) && (usable_stockroom_on_hand <= approved_rop)` (`INV-10` compares usable stockroom on-hand—including reserved/held usable stock—against approved ROP).
4. `net_post_rf_position = eligible_usable_stockroom_on_hand - active_rf_reservations - total_unreserved_approved_rf_demand`
5. `gross_rf_shortage_need = unreserved_approved_rf_shortage`
6. `gross_rop_replenishment_need = (low_stock_breach && approved_target_level != null) ? max(0, approved_target_level - max(0, net_post_rf_position)) : 0`
7. `gross_combined_need = gross_rf_shortage_need + gross_rop_replenishment_need` (strictly non-overlapping: units already consumed by RF reservations and unreserved RF demand are never double-counted with ROP top-up).
8. `total_open_inbound_coverage = open_repf_quantity + open_po_inbound_quantity` (includes active non-draft open RepFs and `issued`/`partially_received` POs; excludes inbound flagged `late`, `disputed`, or `ineligible`, which is surfaced separately in `excluded_inbound_quantity` and `inbound_sources` per `INV-12`).
9. Inbound covers RF shortage first, then ROP top-up:
   - `uncovered_rf_shortage_quantity = max(0, gross_rf_shortage_need - total_open_inbound_coverage)`
   - `uncovered_rop_replenishment_quantity = max(0, gross_rop_replenishment_need - max(0, total_open_inbound_coverage - gross_rf_shortage_need))`
   - `proposed_uncovered_quantity = uncovered_rf_shortage_quantity + uncovered_rop_replenishment_quantity`

### Internal `computeNetShortageSnapshot` transaction boundary

- Owned by Glenn; exposes zero public HTTP routes.
- Called synchronously with a bounded `variant_ids` batch (`1..100`) by `listReplenishmentNeeds` (after resolving the current snapshot-paginated page of up to `limit` active variant IDs) and by Leader's `createRepf`, `replaceRepf`, `submitRepf`, and `reviewRepfBudget` transactions (under Leader's outer MySQL transaction locks so concurrent RepF submissions serialize cleanly against live stock/RF/inbound state).
- Accepts `exclude_repf_id` so replacing, submitting, or budget-reviewing an existing RepF does not count that RepF's own open lines against itself.
- Returns `ready`, `setup_blocked`, or `rejected`; Leader aborts the enclosing write transaction on any non-`ready` outcome.

### 6.2 Triggers, non-overlapping breakdown & duplicate-coverage guard (`PRC-01`–`PRC-03`, `INV-11`–`INV-12`)

- **Strict Stage 2 triggers (`PRC-01`):** `RepfTriggerKind` accepts only `rf_shortage` and `rop_replenishment`. Any `so_shortage` payload fails request validation with `422 invalid_input` pointing to `/lines/{i}/trigger_kind` (and `GET /repfs?trigger_kind=so_shortage` fails with `400 invalid_selector`). `funding_source` is fixed to `chapter_funds`.
- **RF linkage & line-uniqueness validation:** Every `rf_shortage` line requires non-null same-chapter `rf_id` and `rf_line_id` referencing a currently approved (non-canceled, non-fully-issued) internal RF line matching `variant_id` and `unit_id`; ineligible or closed RF lines fail with `409 source_not_eligible` (foreign/unknown IDs return `404 resource_not_found`). Every `rop_replenishment` line requires `rf_id: null`, `rf_line_id: null`, and effective `rop` + `target_level` policies (`require_rop_and_target_policies: true` → `409 setup_blocked` if missing/expired). Within a single RepF (capped at `maxItems: 100` lines to match `computeNetShortageSnapshot`'s `variant_ids: 1..100` bound), duplicate `rf_line_id`s across `rf_shortage` lines and duplicate `(variant_id, rop_replenishment)` lines are rejected with `422 invalid_input`.
- **Multi-trigger RepFs & net-need breakdown:** One RepF may combine `rf_shortage` and `rop_replenishment` lines (even for the same variant). Each line persists its exact server-computed `RepfLineNeedBreakdown` from Glenn's `computeNetShortageSnapshot` without double-counting overlapping shortage and ROP quantities.
- **Duplicate-supply-coverage protection (`INV-12`):** On `createRepf`, `replaceRepf`, `submitRepf`, and `reviewRepfBudget(approve)`, Leader enforces three non-overlapping bounds unless an explicit `additional_purchase_reason` (`category: late_supply | disputed_supply | ineligible_supply`, optional `referenced_source_ids`, non-blank `explanation`) is recorded on every excess line: (a) each `rf_shortage` line's `requested_quantity` cannot exceed its linked RF line's live `unreserved_outstanding_quantity` or `uncovered_rf_shortage_quantity`, and each `rop_replenishment` line's `requested_quantity` cannot exceed `uncovered_rop_replenishment_quantity`; (b) the sum of `requested_quantity` per `(variant_id, trigger_kind)` cannot exceed that trigger's live uncovered quantity (`uncovered_rf_shortage_quantity` / `uncovered_rop_replenishment_quantity`); and (c) the combined sum across both triggers for a `variant_id` cannot exceed `variant_proposed_uncovered_quantity`. Any unapproved excess aborts the transaction with `409 duplicate_supply_coverage`, `failed_intent_committed: false`, and line-level `coverage_conflicts[]` naming the blocking inbound RepF/PO sources.

### 6.3 Authorization, transaction/lock boundaries & committed events

- **`x-stage2-action-duties`:**
  - `repf.read`: `INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`, `BUDGET_APPROVER`, `CHAPTER_HEAD`, `FINANCE`, `INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER`
  - `repf.draft`: `INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`
  - `repf.submit`: `INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`
  - `repf.budget_review`: `BUDGET_APPROVER`, `CHAPTER_HEAD` (strictly independent of `repf.preparer.user_id` → `403 independence_required`; `approve` additionally enforces `DutyGrant.scope.limit_php >= repf.estimated_total_php` → `403 duty_limit_exceeded`)
  - `repf.cancel`: `INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`, `BUDGET_APPROVER`, `CHAPTER_HEAD` (post-approval `approved`/`partially_ordered` narrowed to `BUDGET_APPROVER`/`CHAPTER_HEAD`)
- **MySQL transaction & lock order (Leader owns outer transaction):**
  1. Verify session/MFA, chapter membership, `Origin` + `X-XSRF-TOKEN`, current duty, and non-locking `ActionReceipt` pre-check.
  2. Open MySQL transaction; acquire shared authority/policy locks (`chapter`, `duty_grants`, `reference_series` / `rop` / `target_level` / `quantity_scale`), then lock the target RepF row (`FOR UPDATE` on existing RepF) and sorted chapter variant guard rows (`FOR UPDATE`) to serialize concurrent RepF submissions against the same variant's live uncovered need.
  3. Recheck `ActionReceipt` (`sharedLock`) inside the lock boundary: identical canonical fingerprint replays original `200`/`201` snapshot with `X-Action-Replayed: true`; changed payload aborts with `409 action_key_conflict`.
  4. Verify `expected_version`, `expected_material_version`, status transition, independence, and monetary ceiling (`approve`).
  5. Invoke Glenn's `computeNetShortageSnapshot` synchronously inside the transaction (`exclude_repf_id = repf_id` for existing RepFs); abort on `setup_blocked`, `rejected`, or `duplicate_supply_coverage`.
  6. On first `submitRepf`, lock chapter `REPF` yearly sequence counter (`FOR UPDATE`) and allocate `repf_reference` once-only.
  7. Persist RepF, lines, immutable version snapshot, history entry, `ActionReceipt`, and transactional outbox event `stage2.repf.changed.v1` (Ticket 07 8-field envelope on `private-stage1.chapter.{chapter_id}`), then commit.
- **Action-receipt recovery (`/procurement-operations/by-key` & `/procurement-operations/{operation_id}`):** Strictly limited to the original immutable actor who still holds current chapter membership, `repf.read`, and the originating mutation's current required duty. Unknown, in-flight, foreign-chapter, or other-actor receipts return uniform `404 operation_not_found` (never proof of rollback). Never log query strings containing `action_key`.

### 6.4 Stage 1 `cancelInternalRf` compatibility contract (`PRC-08`)

Stage 1's `POST /api/v1/chapters/{chapter_id}/internal-rfs/{rf_id}/cancel` (`cancelInternalRf`, owned by Glenn) retains its exact Stage 1 HTTP route, request schema, and response schema and **never blocks or deadlocks** on open Stage 2 RepFs or POs:
- Inside `cancelInternalRf`'s MySQL transaction, after updating the RF's remaining unissued line quantity to canceled, lock affected open Stage 2 RepF rows (`status` in `draft`, `pending_budget_review`, `held`, `returned_for_revision`, `approved`, `partially_ordered` in sorted `id` order to prevent deadlocks) and linked open PO rows (Ticket 03).
- Set `linked_demand_canceled = true` and `linked_demand_canceled_at = committed_at` on each affected RepF line and `linked_demand_canceled = true` on the parent RepF, append an immutable `linked_demand_canceled` history entry (incrementing `version` without changing `material_version`), and emit `stage2.repf.changed.v1` on commit.
- Live officer alerts (`GET /alerts`, Ticket 06) immediately surface a derived `demand_changed` alert referencing the flagged RepF/PO so Purchasing and Budget officers can adjust or cancel un-ordered remainders deliberately.

### 11.2 Nested quotations & quotation-policy enforcement (`PRC-05`)

- Quotations nest strictly under an `approved` or `partially_ordered` RepF (other RepF states return `409 source_not_eligible`). Each quotation captures inline supplier metadata (`supplier_name`, `supplier_subtype`, `contact_reference`, optional `supplier_reference`), PHP line pricing (scale 2, rounded half-up per line and summed), lead time, availability, quality/warranty, payment/delivery terms, optional reliability notes, and optional `quotation_attachment` paper evidence bound against `{repf_id}` and verified via Leader's internal `checkEvidenceBinding`.

### 11.3 PO state machine, independence, amendments, short-closure & cancellation (`PRC-06`–`09`, `RCV-06`, `RCV-07`)

- `x-stage2-po-transitions` defines the complete PO state machine (`draft`, `pending_approval`, `returned_for_revision`, `rejected`, `issued`, `partially_received`, `receiving_complete`, `closed_short`, `canceled`).
- `replacePurchaseOrder` (`PUT`) edits `draft` in place and transitions `returned_for_revision` back to `draft` (incrementing both `version` and `material_version`).
- `approvePurchaseOrder` transitions directly from `pending_approval` to `issued` in one atomic MySQL transaction:
  - Enforces strict independence from **both** `po.preparer.user_id` AND originating `repf.preparer.user_id` (`403 independence_required`).
  - Enforces actor `DutyGrant.scope.limit_php >= po.total_amount_php` (`403 duty_limit_exceeded`).
  - Enforces approved RepF budget ceiling (cumulative issued PO totals against the RepF plus this PO `<= repf.approved_budget_php`, else `409 budget_limit_exceeded`) and remaining RepF orderable line quantities (`409 source_not_eligible`).
  - Locks chapter yearly `PO` `reference_series` counter (`FOR UPDATE`; missing/expired policy → `409 setup_blocked`) and allocates `po_reference` once-only.
  - Updates originating RepF line `ordered_quantity` / `remaining_to_order_quantity` (`approved` → `partially_ordered` or `ordered`, bumping RepF `version` with `po_ordered_progress` history entry).
  - Increases `inbound_open_po` projection without modifying `usable_stockroom_on_hand`, `available_to_allocate`, or linked RF shortages (`PRC-07`, `PRC-09`).
- **Optional non-blocking supplier acknowledgment (`POST /purchase-orders/{po_id}/acknowledgment`):** Allowed on `issued` or `partially_received` POs; records supplier confirmation reference, timestamp, revised expected delivery date, notes, and optional `quotation_attachment` paper bound to `{po_id}`. Strictly non-blocking: `PO-RM` receiving never requires prior acknowledgment. Increments `version` only.
- **Controlled post-issuance amendments (`POST /amendments` + `POST /amendments/{amendment_id}/approve`):** Allowed on `issued` or `partially_received` POs with mandatory non-blank `reason` (at most one pending amendment per PO). Re-validates effective `purchasing_quotation_policy` (`409 setup_blocked` / `quotation_policy_unmet` if the amended total enters a higher quote-count tier without sufficient comparable quotes or a permitted waiver), rejects any quantity reduction below live `accepted_quantity` or variant/unit substitution on lines with `accepted_quantity > 0` with `409 po_has_accepted_receipts` (`PRC-08`, `RCV-07`), and rejects totals exceeding approved RepF budget with `409 budget_limit_exceeded`. Amendments take effect only upon independent `approvePurchaseOrderAmendment` (approver holding `BUDGET_APPROVER`/`CHAPTER_HEAD`, intersected with `emergency_waiver.approving_duties` when waiver was used, distinct from amendment proposer, PO preparer, and originating Inventory RepF preparer → `403 independence_required`, within grant ceiling → `403 duty_limit_exceeded`), bumping both `version` and `material_version` and updating `inbound_open_po` and RepF ordered progress atomically.
- **Short-closure & cancellation:**
  - `closeShortPurchaseOrder` (`issued` or `partially_received` → `closed_short`) requires independent `BUDGET_APPROVER`/`CHAPTER_HEAD` (distinct from PO preparer and originating RepF preparer → `403 independence_required`, within actor `DutyGrant.scope.limit_php >= po.total_amount_php` → `403 duty_limit_exceeded`) and mandatory non-blank `reason`; sets `closed_short_quantity = outstanding_quantity` and `outstanding_quantity = 0`, reducing `inbound_open_po` to `0` while preserving `accepted_quantity`, `rejected_or_disputed_quantity`, quarantined lots, and open discrepancies (`RCV-06`).
  - `cancelPurchaseOrder` (`draft`, `returned_for_revision`, or unreceived `issued` → `canceled`) requires mandatory non-blank `reason`; post-issuance cancellation requires independent `BUDGET_APPROVER`/`CHAPTER_HEAD`, retains `po_reference`, restores unreceived ordered quantities on open parent RepFs, and zeroes `inbound_open_po`. Any PO with `accepted_quantity > 0` (including `partially_received`) rejects with `409 po_has_accepted_receipts`.

### 11.4 Stage 1 `/stock*` `inbound_open_po` & `cancelInternalRf` compatibility (`PRC-07`–`09`)

- **Quantity conservation (`PRC-07`):** Every PO line satisfies `ordered_quantity == accepted_quantity + closed_short_quantity + canceled_quantity + outstanding_quantity`. `rejected_or_disputed_quantity` tracks damaged/rejected delivery counts separately and does **not** reduce `outstanding_quantity` (e.g., ordered `100`, accepted `40`, rejected `10` → `outstanding_quantity = 60` until replacement arrives via a new `PO-RM` or formal `close-short`).
- **Glenn `/stock*` `inbound_open_po` alignment:** Stage 1 `GET /stock` and `GET /stock/variants/{variant_id}` (`StockSummary.views.inbound_open_po.quantity`) project the live chapter sum of `outstanding_quantity` across PO lines whose parent PO is `issued` or `partially_received`. `views_are_additive` remains `false`; issuing or amending a PO never changes `usable_stockroom_on_hand`, `available_to_allocate`, or linked RF shortages (`PRC-07`, `PRC-09`).
- **Stage 1 `cancelInternalRf` compatibility (`PRC-08`):** Canceling an unissued RF remainder never blocks on open POs; in the same MySQL transaction, affected open POs (`draft`, `pending_approval`, `returned_for_revision`, `issued`, `partially_received`, locked in sorted `id` order after open RepFs) are flagged with `linked_demand_canceled = true` and `linked_demand_canceled_at = committed_at`, append a `linked_demand_canceled` history entry (bumping `version`, not `material_version`), and emit `stage2.purchase-order.changed.v1` on commit.

### 11.5 Locking order, committed events & downstream acceptance

- **MySQL lock order (Leader owns outer transaction):** Session/MFA/duty/non-locking `ActionReceipt` pre-check → open transaction → shared authority/policy locks (`chapter`, `duty_grants`, `purchasing_quotation_policy`, `reference_series(PO)`, `quantity_scale`) → `FOR UPDATE` on originating RepF (when creating/approving/amending/canceling PO) → `FOR UPDATE` on target quotation / PO row → `ActionReceipt::sharedLock()` replay check → `checkEvidenceBinding` (when optional paper evidence is supplied) → `FOR UPDATE` on chapter `PO` yearly counter (only on `approvePurchaseOrder`) → persist changes, immutable version snapshot, history, `ActionReceipt`, and transactional outbox event `stage2.purchase-order.changed.v1` (plus `stage2.repf.changed.v1` when RepF ordered progress changes), then commit.
- **Downstream HTTP/MySQL acceptance obligations:** Verify tier-based quotation counts, emergency waiver approving-duty checks, `409 setup_blocked` on missing/expired policy, `409 quotation_policy_unmet`, PO approval independence against both PO preparer and originating RepF preparer (`403 independence_required`), grant monetary ceiling (`403 duty_limit_exceeded`), approved RepF budget ceiling (`409 budget_limit_exceeded`), once-only concurrent `PO` reference allocation, optional non-blocking supplier acknowledgment, post-issuance amendment blocking below `accepted_quantity` (`409 po_has_accepted_receipts`), `close-short` preserving rejected/quarantined quantities, Stage 1 `/stock*` `inbound_open_po` reflection without inflating usable stock, and `/procurement-operations/*` recovery across all 22 procurement mutations.

- **Stage 1 `/opening-rms` untouched (`RCV-01`, `RCV-10`):** Stage 1 `/api/v1/chapters/{chapter_id}/opening-rms*` and `prepareOpeningInventory` remain unchanged for one-time IPC opening-stock baselines. Stage 2 `/api/v1/chapters/{chapter_id}/receiving-reports` accepts strictly `source_kind: po | da` and shares only the chapter's yearly `RM` `reference_series` counter allocated atomically on `postReceivingReport`.
- **Action duties (`x-stage2-action-duties`):** `receiving.read` (`RECEIVING`, `INVENTORY`, `PURCHASING`, `BUDGET_APPROVER`, `CHAPTER_HEAD`, `FINANCE`, `INDEPENDENT_REVIEWER`), `receiving.draft` (`RECEIVING`, `INVENTORY`), `receiving.inspect` / `receiving.match_evidence` / `receiving.post` (`RECEIVING`), `receiving.cancel` (`RECEIVING`, `CHAPTER_HEAD`), `discrepancy.manage` (`RECEIVING`, `PURCHASING`), `discrepancy.resolve` (`RECEIVING`, `PURCHASING`, `INVENTORY`, `CHAPTER_HEAD`), and `receiving.hold_manage` (`RECEIVING`, `INVENTORY`, `CHAPTER_HEAD`). History/version reads (`listReceivingReportHistory`, `readReceivingReportVersion`, `listReceivingHoldHistory`) additionally require current `history.read` and applicable `evidence.read` when disclosing evidence metadata.

### 12.2 Receiving Report state machine, inspection rules & source/overdelivery guards (`RCV-01`–`04`, `RCV-07`)

- **State machine (`x-stage2-receiving-transitions`):**
  - `draft` → `inspected` (`inspectReceivingReport`) | `draft` (`replaceReceivingReport`) | `canceled` (`cancelReceivingReport`)
  - `inspected` → `evidence_matched` (`matchReceivingReportEvidence`) | `draft` (`replaceReceivingReport`, bumping `material_version` and clearing `inspection` + `snapshot`) | `canceled`
  - `evidence_matched` → `posted` (`postReceivingReport`, allocating shared yearly `RM` reference atomically) | `draft` (`replaceReceivingReport`, bumping `material_version` and automatically invalidating `inspection`, `snapshot`, and `evidence_acknowledgment`) | `canceled`
  - `posted` and `canceled` are immutable terminal states (`409 invalid_transition` on mutation).
- **Source eligibility (`409 source_not_eligible`):** `source_kind: po` requires a same-chapter PO in `issued` or `partially_received` status plus non-blank `supplier_dr_reference` and `supplier_dr_date`; `source_kind: da` requires a same-chapter DA in `acknowledged` or `partially_received` status (and `supplier_dr_reference: null`, `supplier_dr_date: null`). If `originating_hold_id` is supplied, the hold must belong to the same chapter, be in `linked_to_source` status, and match the same `source_kind` and `source_id`.
- **Inspection & exact decimal rules (`RCV-02`, `RCV-03`, `RCV-07`):** Inspection captures `inspector`, optional `technical_or_requesting_representative`, `inspected_at`, `inspection_notes`, and exact variant-scale line counts satisfying `delivered_quantity == accepted_quantity + damaged_quantity + rejected_quantity` (zero silent rounding), `accepted_value_php == round_half_up(accepted_quantity * unit_valuation_php, 2)`, mandatory `discrepancy_notes` whenever `damaged_quantity > 0` or `rejected_quantity > 0`, and exact `unit_valuation_php` parity with the approved PO line `unit_price_php` or acknowledged DA line `unit_value_php`. For `source_kind: po`, any line whose `variant_id` or `unit_id` differs from the PO line or whose `accepted_quantity` exceeds the PO line's current `outstanding_quantity` fails with `409 substitution_or_overdelivery_blocked` until an approved PO amendment updates the source. For `source_kind: da`, any variant/unit mismatch or `accepted_quantity` exceeding the DA line's `outstanding_quantity` fails with `409 substitution_or_overdelivery_blocked` (DAs have no PO amendments; excess donated goods require a separate acknowledged DA).
- **Signed paper evidence (`po_supplier_dr` / `da_receipt`):** `inspectReceivingReport` freezes the canonical `ReceivingReportEvidenceMaterial` projection and computes `snapshot.sha256` (target purpose `po_supplier_dr` for `po`, `da_receipt` for `da`). `matchReceivingReportEvidence` verifies exact `snapshot_sha256` and uploaded immutable bytes via Leader's internal `checkEvidenceBinding`, and `postReceivingReport` re-fences both `acknowledgment_id` and `checkEvidenceBinding` inside the commit transaction.

### 12.3 Unified Efren internal posting participant `prepareReceivingInventory` (`RCV-03`–`05`, `INV-14`, ADR 0004)

- **Zero public route:** Exposed strictly under `x-stage2-receiving-participants` (parameterized by `source_kind: po_rm | da_rm`); `/prepare-receiving` is forbidden as a public path.
- **Transactional execution:** Runs synchronously inside Leader's MySQL commit on `postReceivingReport` strictly after Leader locks the chapter's `RM` `reference_series`, source PO/DA, and RM, re-fences authority/policies/evidence/source allowance, and allocates the shared yearly `RM` reference.
- **Lot, discrepancy & source-progress effects:**
  - `accepted_quantity > 0` → creates `condition: usable`, `custody: stockroom` lot(s) (`StockSource.kind: po_rm | da_rm`) and `accepted_receipt` movement(s). For `po_rm`, `restrictions.state` is `unrestricted` and `immediately_allocatable: true` (`RCV-04`, `INV-09`). For `da_rm`, each lot inherits the DA line's current `BoundedRestriction`: `unrestricted` and `bounded` lots are `immediately_allocatable: true` for eligible demand, whereas ADR 0004 `uninterpreted` lots are physically on-hand in stockroom stock with `immediately_allocatable: false` until `resolveDonationRestriction` + `applyLotRestrictionResolution` resolves them. Multiple lots sharing one SKU/variant preserve independent restrictions.
  - `damaged_quantity > 0` → creates `condition: quarantined`, `custody: stockroom`, `immediately_allocatable: false` lot(s) and `quarantined_receipt` movement(s); `rejected_quantity > 0` → creates `condition: rejected`, `custody: stockroom`, `immediately_allocatable: false` lot(s) and `rejected_receipt` movement(s) (`INV-14`). Every line with `damaged_quantity > 0` or `rejected_quantity > 0` emits a `ReceivingDiscrepancySeed` persisted by Leader as an `open` `ReceivingDiscrepancy`.
  - **Source progress arithmetic (worked example `PO 100 / RM 40 accepted + 10 rejected`):** PO line `accepted_quantity` increases by `40`, `rejected_or_disputed_quantity` increases by `10`, and `outstanding_quantity` decreases only by accepted (`100 - 40 = 60`), keeping PO status `partially_received` with `60` open inbound (`RCV-05`) while usable stockroom on-hand increases immediately by `40` (`RCV-04`) and rejected stock increases by `10` with an open discrepancy (`INV-14`). When all PO lines reach `outstanding_quantity == 0` via accepted receipts, PO transitions to `receiving_complete` while retaining any open discrepancies independently. For DA, `received_quantity` increases by `accepted_quantity` and `outstanding_quantity` decreases by `accepted_quantity` (`acknowledged -> partially_received | received`); DA never enters PO open-balance comparison (`A-07`).

### 12.4 Receiving discrepancy loops (`RCV-05`–`06`, `INV-14`, `S3-21`)

- **State machine (`x-stage2-discrepancy-transitions`):** `open` → `supplier_notified` (`notifySupplierOfReceivingDiscrepancy`, PO only; DA returns `409 source_not_eligible`) | `under_review` (`resolveReceivingDiscrepancy` with `action: start_review`) → `resolved` (`resolveReceivingDiscrepancy` with `action: resolve` from `supplier_notified` or `under_review`). Once `resolved`, further transitions return `409 discrepancy_already_resolved`.
- **Evidence & replacement rules:** Final resolution requires `resolution_kind` (`supplier_replacement_received | returned_to_supplier | credit_or_short_closed` for `po`; `donated_declined_or_disposed` for `da`), mandatory `reason`, `quarantined_lot_disposition`, and exact signed `receiving_discrepancy` paper evidence verified via `checkEvidenceBinding` against canonical `ReceivingDiscrepancyEvidenceMaterial`. Supplier replacement goods MUST arrive through a **new** `PO-RM` against the still-open PO referencing `resolves_discrepancy_id` (immutable posted RM; never edit a posted RM), and `supplier_replacement_received` requires `replacement_rm_id` referencing that posted replacement `PO-RM`.
- **No silent quarantine clearance (`RCV-06`):** `quarantined_lots_auto_cleared_on_source_close` is permanently `false`. Closing or short-closing a PO (`closeShortPurchaseOrder`) or DA (`closeShortDonation`) never resolves open discrepancies or converts quarantined/rejected lots into usable stock.

### 12.5 Missing-source delivery holds (`RCV-01`, `RCV-10`, `S3-15`)

- **State machine (`x-stage2-hold-transitions`):** `held` (`createReceivingHold`) → `linked_to_source` (`linkReceivingHoldSource`, attaching verified approved PO+DR or acknowledged DA only) | `rejected_returned` (`rejectReturnReceivingHold`, requiring signed `missing_source_hold` paper evidence verified via `checkEvidenceBinding` against `ReceivingHoldRejectionEvidenceMaterial`). Already resolved holds return `409 hold_already_resolved`.
- **Hard invariants:** `usable_stock_posted` is permanently `false` and `ipc_creation_prohibited` is permanently `true`. Holds keep undocumented arrivals physically isolated outside usable stock, strictly forbid IPC creation or IPC linking (`source_kind` accepts only `po | da`), and unlock standard receiving only through `createReceivingReport` with `originating_hold_id` after `linked_to_source`.

### 12.6 Locking order, recovery, committed events & downstream acceptance

- **Locking order (deadlock-free):** Chapter context & authority/policies → `reference_series` (`RM`, on `postReceivingReport`) → `ReceivingHold` (if linked) → source `PurchaseOrder` or `Donation` (and source lines) → `ReceivingReport` → `ReceivingDiscrepancy` → Efren stockroom lots/version (`prepareReceivingInventory`) → `checkEvidenceBinding` → action receipt + transactional outbox.
- **Recovery (`/receiving-operations/by-key` & `/receiving-operations/{operation_id}`):** Covers all 11 unsafe Ticket 05 mutations (`ReceivingOperationName`) using discriminated `ReceivingOperationReceipt` (`receiving_report`, `receiving_discrepancy`, `receiving_hold`), strictly limited to the original immutable actor who retains current chapter membership, `receiving.read`, and the originating mutation's required duty.
- **Committed events (ticket 07 registry):** `postReceivingReport` emits `stage2.receiving-report.changed.v1`, `stage1.stock-lot.changed.v1`, `stage2.purchase-order.changed.v1` or `stage2.donation.changed.v1`, and `stage2.receiving-discrepancy.changed.v1` (when discrepancies are created); other RM/discrepancy/hold mutations emit their respective committed `stage2.receiving-*.changed.v1` notices on `private-stage1.chapter.{chapter_id}`.
- **Downstream HTTP/MySQL acceptance obligations:** Verify `PO 100 / RM 40 accepted + 10 rejected` arithmetic (usable `+40` immediately allocatable under `INV-09`, rejected `+10` + open discrepancy, PO `partially_received` with `60` open), unamended overdelivery above `60` and variant/unit substitution blocking (`409 substitution_or_overdelivery_blocked`), material edit invalidating `evidence_matched` binding back to `draft`, supplier replacement via a **new** `PO-RM` referencing `resolves_discrepancy_id` + `receiving_discrepancy` evidence resolution, `DA-RM` posting `bounded` and ADR 0004 `uninterpreted` lots sharing a SKU, `closeShortPurchaseOrder`/`closeShortDonation` preserving quarantined/rejected lots (`RCV-06`), missing-source hold zero usable stock + IPC prohibition + `link-source` / `reject-return` with `missing_source_hold` evidence, concurrent `RM` reference allocation once-only uniqueness shared with Stage 1 `/opening-rms`, and `/receiving-operations/*` lost-response recovery.
